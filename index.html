WHAT RARTIST IS

We are building a gallery without the room. Each season we release a single, tightly edited collection of prints — thirty works, no more — sourced from painters, photographers and printmakers we have followed for years.

Every edition is printed on cotton rag, numbered, and retired for good once it sells out. No infinite catalogue, no algorithmic recommendations, no art chosen because it matches a sofa.
